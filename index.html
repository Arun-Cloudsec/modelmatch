Why this exists

Every "best LLM" article is already out of date.

In the last 18 months I've been asked the same question by every customer I work with: "which model should we use?"

And every single time, the honest answer required me to cross-check pricing pages, official papers, regional availability, data-residency fine print, and at least three benchmark leaderboards. For one question. That is broken.

Worse: most of the public comparison sites are either built by a specific vendor (so Claude always wins, or GPT always wins, depending on who paid), or they stop at "here's a table of models" without ever asking the real questions — what's it going to cost monthly at my actual volume? Is the inference region inside my country? What's the open-source alternative if the budget is cut?

ModelMatch is the tool I wish existed every time someone said "we need to pick an LLM by Friday."

So I built it. Vendor-neutral. No affiliate links. No hidden partner tiers. Pricing sourced directly from provider documentation and sovereignty classifications done country-by-country from actual regulatory documents — not marketing pages.

The feature you won't find anywhere else

Sovereignty, by country.

🛡️ Unique to ModelMatch

The most common compliance trap isn't where your data is stored.
It's where inference actually runs.

Azure OpenAI in UAE North can execute inference in West Europe during peak load. Claude on GCP "available in Dubai" may route to Frankfurt. A vendor's local office is not the same as local compute.

ModelMatch classifies every model against 12 jurisdictions — UAE, Saudi, Germany, Singapore, India, US, UK, Australia, Japan, France, Canada, Brazil — with four residency states: in-country sovereign, in-country compliant, may-route-outside, or processed-abroad. Plus a self-host flag for open-weight models you can deploy anywhere.

This is the view a government, bank, or healthcare team actually needs — and the one every other comparison tool skips.
